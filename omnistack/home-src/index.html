FAQ

Q1How long does a project take?

A SaaS website usually takes four to six weeks. A first app release takes ten to fourteen. You get a dated plan before we start.

Q2Do you build for iOS and Android?

Yes. Cross-platform or fully native, chosen per project, with the trade-off explained in writing.

Q3Can you run our social media?

Yes. Monthly strategy, content and ads, with a report tied to signups and installs, not vanity numbers.

Q4What does it cost?

Builds are priced after a short discovery call, and the number we quote is the number you pay. Social is a flat monthly fee.

Q5Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Code, designs, store and social accounts sit in your name from day one.

Q6How do we start?

Send a short brief from the mug on the desk. We reply within two working days with questions or a call slot.
